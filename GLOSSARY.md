# Pwnagotchi HGSEC

Terms for the customized Pwnagotchi device and its relationship with Pwnabase.

## Language

**Capture**:
A recording of wireless packets collected during an authorized security study. A capture may contain one or more handshakes, or none.
_Avoid_: Hash, password

**Button action**:
An operation assigned to a physical control on the device's LCD HAT.

**Deauthentication control**:
A button action that changes whether the device performs deauthentication during wireless activity.
